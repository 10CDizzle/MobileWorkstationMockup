Reasonable questions.

We’re pre-launch, so some of these are plans rather than promises. Marked as such.

What if my hotel won’t hold packages?

Most business hotels hold guest packages as a matter of course, and we confirm it with the property before anything ships. If a hotel won’t, we’ll tell you before your trip rather than after, and you won’t be billed for that stay. Working out the edge cases is exactly what the founding group is for.

Does it work with a Mac and a locked-down work PC?

That’s the plan: a universal USB-C dock, one cable, no software to install and nothing to approve with IT. Older or restricted laptops sometimes cap you at one external screen. We’ll include the adapters that work around it where we can, and we’ll be upfront where we can’t.

What if something breaks while I’m on the road?

You tell us, and a replacement ships to your next hotel. You don’t pay for normal wear or a monitor that dies on its own. Lost or badly damaged kit is the one case we’d charge for, and we’ll publish that number plainly before anyone signs up.

How far ahead do I need to book?

We’re targeting a few days’ notice for a standard delivery, with same-week changes on the unlimited plan. Consulting itineraries move constantly and a service that can’t keep up with a Tuesday reroute isn’t worth paying for.

Which cities will you cover?

We’re starting with New York, Chicago, Dallas, Atlanta and the Bay Area, because that’s where the most consulting nights are. Tell us where you actually fly in the form. The waitlist decides what opens next.

Can my company pay for it?

You can expense it from day one with the monthly invoice and emailed receipts. Direct billing to a firm, with multiple consultants under one account, is planned for launch.

Is the equipment cleaned between users?

Yes. Every kit comes back to us between stays rather than hopping guest to guest. It gets inspected, wiped down, tested and repacked in fresh foam liners. Anything that looks tired gets pulled out of rotation.

When do you launch?

Honestly: when enough people on this list say they’d pay for it. We’re validating demand before buying a single monitor. Join the list and you’ll hear directly from us: first about the pilot, then about pricing that stays locked for founding members.
