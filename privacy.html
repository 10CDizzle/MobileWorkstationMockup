Privacy

The short version

We collect what you type into the waitlist form, we keep it in a private spreadsheet, and we use it to email you about this product. That is the whole arrangement.

What we collect

Only what the form asks for: your email address, and optionally your first name, role, how many hotel nights you do, whether your employer would reimburse this, which plan you would pick, what you would pay, your must-have equipment, and any notes you leave.

The form also records which link brought you here — for example ?utm_source=linkedin or ?ref=friend — and which pricing card you clicked, if you clicked one. That tells us where interest comes from. It is not tied to anything outside this page.

Where it goes

Submitting the form posts your answers to a Google Form, which writes them to a Google Sheet we control. Google processes that data under its own terms as our service provider. Nobody else is given access, and we do not sell, rent or share the list.

What we do with it

We read it in aggregate to decide whether to build this, what belongs in the case, and what it should cost. We will email you about the launch, pilot invitations and founding-member pricing. We will not add you to an unrelated newsletter, and every email we send will have an unsubscribe link.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no advertising or tracking scripts. Nothing on the page follows you anywhere else.

Keeping and deleting it

We keep responses while we are still validating this idea, and no longer than two years. Email hello@casework.co and we will delete your row and confirm it, usually within a few days. You can ask for a copy of what we hold the same way.

Changes

If this changes before launch, the updated version lives at this address. We are pre-launch and this page describes a waitlist, not a customer account.
